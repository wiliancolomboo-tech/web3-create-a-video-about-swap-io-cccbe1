    %% Flow of Initialize
    Maker -->|1. Sign & Submit tx| InitIx
    InitIx --> SignerCheck
    SignerCheck -->|Pass| PDAProof
    PDAProof -->|Derived & Initialized| SwapState
    InitIx -->|2. Transfer Collateral| CPI_Deposit
    CPI_Deposit --> VaultA

    %% Flow of Execute
    Taker -->|3. Route Counter-Asset & Fulfill| ExecIx
    ExecIx --> ClockGuard
    ClockGuard -->|Valid| BalanceGuard
    BalanceGuard -->|Valid| SlippageGuard
    SlippageGuard -->|Settlement Path| CPI_PayMaker
    CPI_PayMaker -->|Credit B| Maker
    ExecIx -->|Sign with PDA seeds| CPI_PayTaker
    CPI_PayTaker -->|Credit A| Taker

    %% Flow of Cancel
    Maker -->|Emergency Cancel| CancelIx
    CancelIx --> SignerCheck
    CancelIx --> CPI_Refund
    CPI_Refund --> Maker
